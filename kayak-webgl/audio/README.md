# Riverlight audio

The recordings are available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `hull.mp3` is a quiet water loop that fades in with the kayak's speed. It is trimmed, filtered, level adjusted and crossfaded from [Paddling a Kayak by Danjocross](https://freesound.org/people/Danjocross/sounds/503208/).
- `paddle-1.mp3`, `paddle-2.mp3` and `paddle-3.mp3` are individual paddle splashes. They are trimmed, filtered, level matched and faded from [Water Paddle Splashing rowing in a canoe by egeexyz](https://freesound.org/people/egeexyz/sounds/702386/).
- `wings.mp3` is a trimmed and softened excerpt from [Pigeon flock fly away by TRP](https://freesound.org/people/TRP/sounds/616623/).
- `river-birds.mp3` is a short excerpt recorded by the Río Manso, with the low water noise filtered down. It comes from [Birds and distant river by fran_marenco](https://freesound.org/people/fran_marenco/sounds/796519/).
